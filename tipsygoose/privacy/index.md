# Tipsy Goose — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 28 October 2026 · **App:** Tipsy Goose («В гуся») for Android and iOS · **Developer:** Evgenii Chupir (Umptek), Cyprus · **Contact:** umptek.dev@gmail.com

## In short

Tipsy Goose has no account, no ads, no analytics and no third-party tracking. The app has no internet access of its own: your entries stay on your device, and the app sends nothing anywhere by itself. The developer receives information only if you choose to send it — for example, a feedback email.

## What the app stores on your device

- **Drink entries:** category, volume, alcohol strength, date and time with your time zone offset.
- **App settings:** whether onboarding is complete, the date your tracking started, the start of the free trial and whether the full version is unlocked.
- **A technical error log:** short messages about app errors, without your entries.
- **In test (beta) versions only:** a log of trial-offer events — dates, days of the trial, which screens were shown and which buttons were pressed.

This data is stored only on your device. The developer has no access to it.

## Device backups

If backup is turned on on your device, Android or iOS may include the app's data in your Google or iCloud backup. Backups are handled by Google or Apple under their own privacy policies and your device settings. You can turn backup off in your device settings.

## Save to file

"Save to file" creates a file with all your entries on your device or in a place you choose. The app doesn't send this file anywhere. You decide where to keep it and whom to share it with. Anyone who has the file can read your entries.

## Send feedback

When you tap "Send feedback", the app opens your email app or the share menu with a prepared message. Below your text it contains the app version, your operating system and device model, the app language, the technical error log and, in test versions, the trial-offer log. It doesn't contain your drink entries. You see the whole message and can edit or delete any part of it before sending. Nothing is sent unless you send it.

If you send it, the developer receives your email address and the message. They are used only to reply to you and to fix problems, are not shared with anyone, and are deleted within 12 months, or earlier on request.

## App stores

Google Play and the App Store process data when you download and update the app, and may share crash reports and diagnostics with the developer if you allowed this in your device settings. The developer sees these reports in aggregated form, without your identity. On Google Play, the app is protected against redistribution by Google Play's automatic protection, which checks that the app was installed from Google Play; the developer has turned off the sharing of telemetry from this check. Google and Apple process this data under their own privacy policies.

## What we don't do

No account or sign-up. No advertising. No analytics. No tracking across apps or websites. No sale or sharing of personal data.

## Deleting your data

- "Delete all entries" in the app's settings erases your entries.
- Deleting the app erases all of its data from the device. Copies in device backups and saved files are managed by you.
- To delete a feedback email you sent, write to umptek.dev@gmail.com.

## Age and health

The app is intended for adults (18+). It is not a medical app and not a breathalyzer: it doesn't determine intoxication, blood alcohol concentration or fitness to drive, and it doesn't give medical advice.

## Your rights

If you are in the European Union or a similar jurisdiction, you can ask for access to, correction or deletion of personal data the developer holds about you (in practice, only feedback emails you sent), and you may lodge a complaint with a data protection authority — in Cyprus, the Commissioner for Personal Data Protection.

## Changes

If this policy changes — for example, when in-app purchases are added — the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date.
