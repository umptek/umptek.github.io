# Tipsy Goose («В гуся»)

A playful alcohol tracker for Android and iOS. Log what you drink; the goose reacts to it. History, a calendar and statistics for a week, a month and a year. No account, no ads, no analytics: your entries stay on your device.

Tipsy Goose is not a medical app and not a breathalyzer. For adults (18+).
